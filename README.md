Small tools. No dependencies. Free to use.

🌉 **[wobbly](https://github.com/KaizenShogun/wobbly)** — open it in a browser and
make the Millennium Bridge shake. A crowd walking *out* of step runs away with it anyway.

9,852 real cases where a `.gitignore` matcher can disagree with git. It shows you which ones.

🎲 **[streak](https://github.com/KaizenShogun/streak)** — skill, or luck wearing a
costume? The exact probability of your longest run, in one file.
